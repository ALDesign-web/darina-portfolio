About Darina

A Little About Me

Hi! I’m Darina Kyrychenko, a designer from Ukraine, currently based in Kyiv.

For me, design is more than just a profession. It’s a way of thinking and seeing familiar things from a different perspective. I guess we were meant to find each other.

Outside of design, I love reading fiction and psychology books. I’m also into sports, especially yoga, Pilates, and strength training. Although I consider eating pizza a sport of its own. When no one’s watching, I love dancing and singing at the top of my lungs.

My family and friends mean the world to me. They’re my biggest source of support and inspiration. I also have my own lucky charm in life — my daemon, a black cat named Cash.

How I Work

I’m driven by curiosity, openness to new ideas, and continuous growth. I enjoy exploring new tools, trying different approaches, experimenting with visual concepts, and stepping outside my comfort zone.

I approach projects not only from a designer’s perspective but also with an understanding of their purpose and the results they aim to achieve. For me, design is not just about creating something visually appealing, but about developing thoughtful solutions that communicate ideas and support broader goals.

I believe in teamwork, open communication, and constructive feedback. The best results come from people who listen to each other, share ideas, and work together towards a common goal.
